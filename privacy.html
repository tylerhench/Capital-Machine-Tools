Privacy Policy

Capital Machine Tools keeps this simple: we only collect what you choose to send us, and we use it only to answer you.

What we collect

Contact form. When you send the form on our Contact page, your name, company, email, phone number and message are emailed to us through our form service, Web3Forms. We use them to reply to you and do not sell or share them.

Phone and email. If you call or email us, we use your details only to answer you and to do business with you.

What we don’t do

We don’t use advertising cookies, and we don’t sell, rent or trade personal information.

Videos and other services

Some machine pages include YouTube videos. They use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode, and nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. After that, YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.

Our website is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes basic technical information (such as your IP address) to deliver and protect the site.

eBay

We list some of our machines on eBay. Our website connects to our own eBay seller account only, to create and update our listings. It does not collect or store information about eBay buyers.
