About Us

Capital Machine Tools has over 35 years of experience buying and selling metalworking machine tools.

We handle CNC lathes, vertical machining centers, boring mills, surface grinders, vertical and horizontal mills, engine lathes and just about all related shop equipment.

In addition, we have a 50,000 square foot warehouse for storage and can easily run most machines under power for your inspection.

Inspection & Storage

Buying used equipment sight unseen is a risk. We keep our inventory under one roof so you can see a machine run before you commit to it. Call ahead and we will have it powered up when you arrive.
