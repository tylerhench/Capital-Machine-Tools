About Us

What We Handle

Based in Fairfield, CT, Capital Machine Tools has over 35 years of experience buying and selling metalworking machine tools. We handle CNC lathes, vertical machining centers, boring mills, surface grinders, vertical and horizontal mills, engine lathes and just about all related shop equipment.

Inspection & Storage

Buying used equipment sight unseen is a risk. We keep our inventory under one roof in a 50,000 square foot warehouse in Bridgeport, CT, and can run most machines under power, so you can see a machine run before you commit to it. Call ahead and we will have it powered up when you arrive.
